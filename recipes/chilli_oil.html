Chilli Oil

Description

A wonderfull seasoning to pair with oriental dishes, like tteokbokki and lamen, very spicy and flavorfull.

Preparation Time: 10 min

Cooking Time: 10 min

Servings: A lot

Ingedients

  • 4 Garlic Clover
  • 2 tbsp Sasame Seeds
  • 1 tbsp Paprika
  • 1 tbsp Ginger Powder
  • 2 tbsp Sugar
  • 2tbsp Chilli Pepper
  • 200 ml Sunflower Oil
  • Salt and MSG to taste

Steps

  1. Mix all the dry ingredients ins a heat resistant container.
  2. Heat the oil until boiling point.
  3. Pour the hot oil over the dry spices, mix and store.
