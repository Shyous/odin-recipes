Teokbokki

Description

Preparation Time:

Cooking Time:

Servings:

Ingredients

Dought

  • 1 cup Glutinous Rice Flour
  • ¾ cup Rice Flour
  • 1 tsp Salt
  • 1 cup hot water

Sauce

  • 2 chopped Onions
  • 2 cups Dashi Broth
  • 4 Garlic Clovers
  • 1 ½ tbsp Brown Sugar
  • 1 tbsp Gochugaru
  • 2 tbsp Gochujang
  • 1 tbsp Shoyu
  • 1 tbsp Sesame Oil
  • 2 boiled Eggs
  • 1 Chive Stalk

Steps

  1. Join the flouers and the salt. Add half of the hot water, mix and add the other half of the water little by litte.
  2. Mix well and cut the dought em 8 slices.
  3. Make little rolls and cut the rice cakes into little cute cilinders.
  4. Boil the rice cakes until they float, reserve.
  5. In a frying pan, brown the onions in the sesame oil, after, brown the garlic.
  6. Ass the rice cakes (tteoks), the dashi broth, gochugaru, gochujang, brown sugar and shoyu. Mix and let ir boil.
  7. Low the fire, put the chive e cook for more 5 minutos.
  8. Serve and add the eggs e sesame seeds on top.
