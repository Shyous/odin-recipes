Sarraceno Bread

Description

Not the best, but a very healthy bread suited for people with gluten intolerance or diabetes. You don`t need any tools just a bowl, a spoon and a baking dish.

Preparation Time: 10 minutes

Cooking Time: 1 hour 10 minutes

Servings: 12 slices

Ingredients

  • 200 g Sarraceno Flour
  • 100 g Almond Flour
  • 4 tbsp Chia Seeds
  • 4 tbsp Olive Oil
  • 450 ml Water
  • 1 tsp Salt
  • 12 g Baking Powder
  • All kinds of seeds (Sunflower, pumpkin , etc..)

Steps

  1. Mix the chia seeds with 250ml of water and let ir rest for 15 minutes.
  2. Mix the sarraceno flour with the almond flour, baking soda and salt.
  3. Add 200ml of water, the olive oil and the chia gel form step 1 and mix wel.
  4. Let de dought rest for 10 minutes, while the oven pre-heats at 170 degress celsius.
  5. Place the dought in a baking dish and add the seeds on top. Bake in the oven for 1 hour and 10 minutes. Make the toothpick test to see if it`s evenly baked.
  6. Let it cool before serving.
